About me

why a we eye

Whyaweeye is a creative name derived from a fragment of my name, [YAVANIKA].

My current work is primarily focused on hand embroidery, exploring texture, patience, and details. Over the years, I have also experimented with mandala dotting, paper quilling, poetry and much more, each medium shaping the way I approach creativity. [VIEW GALLERY ↗]
I’m drawn to slow, handmade processes where patience and repetition shape the final piece. Much of my work grows from curiosity, experimentation, and quiet observation.

This website serves as both an archive of my evolving creative practice and a space to shop selected artworks.
